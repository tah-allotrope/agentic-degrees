# Notes: Working Preferences

Working preferences that shape lesson design and study:

- **Sessions:** 60–75 minute morning sessions, plus one 2–4 h weekend block; evenings are volatile.
- **Language:** English, for every artifact.
- **Pedagogy:** retrieval-practice-first — every lesson ends with a quiz that must be answered
  without notes before new material begins.
- **Videos:** streamed from YouTube only, never downloaded; lessons link the relevant lecture as the
  primary source.
- **Examples:** prefers worked numeric examples over prose; every abstract rule gets at least one
  concrete numeric check.
- **Curriculum tree is read-only:** never write into `mit-ocw-curriculum/`; extract zips into
  `teach-workspace/work/<course>/`.
- **Defer, don't quit:** any unit stalling more than one week is logged in `PROGRESS.md` under
  `## Deferrals` with a re-entry plan, never silently abandoned.
