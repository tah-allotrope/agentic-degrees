# Mission: Battery-Energy-Storage and Grid Electrical Engineering Fluency

## Why

To read a battery-energy-storage or grid one-line diagram, size and sanity-check power-electronics
and machine specifications, and write the computational models that support those decisions —
without deferring to a consultant. The electrical-engineering core (6.01SC, 6.002) builds the
circuit and control foundation; the software track (6.0002) builds the computational modelling
skill.

## Success looks like

- Analyse an arbitrary resistive network by the node method under time pressure.
- Predict the transient and frequency response of a first- and second-order circuit.
- Design a proportional controller and state its stability condition.
- Build a state-machine model of a physical system in Python.
- Run a Monte Carlo simulation and state a confidence interval correctly.

## Constraints

- 12–14 focused study hours per week, 60/40 EE/SE split.
- Morning sessions (60–75 min) are the reliable slot; evenings are volatile.
- An infant at home (born May 2026); sleep is precious and schedules slip.
- Window closes Sun 2026-10-11; every gate must be attempted before then or deferred in writing.

## Out of scope

- Downloading lecture videos (streamed from YouTube only).
- Running the full 6.0001 course (one Python primer lesson only).
- Any paid credential, certificate, tutor, or graded feedback service.
- Modifying anything under `mit-ocw-curriculum/` — read-only study material.
- Courses 6.005 and 6.824 (thin OCW material).
- Completing all 19 courses in this window (Phases 2–5 are sketched, not executed).
